What we do

Every detail.
Fully organized.

We handle everything so you can focus entirely on the music. Whether you arrive with a full itinerary or just a date — we build the rest.

🏛️

Venue securing

We select and book the right Budapest stage for your group — from intimate churches to grand concert halls — matched to your audience size and musical style.

📣

Full promotion

Posters, flyers, online advertising, and our own audience network. We fill the seats. Your performance deserves a full house.

🚌

Logistics & transport

Instrument transport, bus permits for city-centre access, rehearsal coordination, and timing down to the minute.

📋

Complete administration

Permit handling, detailed cost reports, program translation into Hungarian, and continuous status updates — no financial surprises, ever.

🤝

On-site presence

We attend every rehearsal, church service, and concert. Someone you trust is always in the room — ready to solve anything, right when it matters.

⚡

Last-minute requests

Things change on tour. We know Budapest, we know the venues, and we know who to call. Late requests are not problems — they are just part of the job.
